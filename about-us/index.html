About Twin Tech India Pvt. Ltd.

Leading manufacturer of industrial aerosol sprays, car care products and self-adhesive tapes — delivering consistent quality and performance for over 25 years.

Company Overview

TWIN TECH INDIA PVT. LTD.

TWIN TECH INDIA PVT. LTD

Industrial Aerosol Spray, Auto Car Care & Adhesive Tape Manufacturer

Twin Tech India Pvt. Ltd. is a leading Industrial Aerosol Spray, Auto Car Care & Adhesive Tape Manufacturer - In House, offering a comprehensive range of high-performance aerosol solutions, professional car care products, and self-adhesive tapes for industrial and consumer applications.

The company was established in the year 2000 by our Managing Director, Mr. P. K. Sharma (Mechanical Engineer), with a vision to deliver high-quality industrial and consumer maintenance solutions through strong technical expertise and innovation.

"With over 25 years of industry experience, Twin Tech India Pvt. Ltd. has built a strong reputation for quality, consistency, and customer satisfaction. Our commitment to excellence, advanced in-house manufacturing capabilities, and continuous improvement has enabled us to serve diverse industries across domestic and international markets with reliability and efficiency."

Our Brands

We manufacture and market our products under the following registered brands:

SPRAYZET

Under the SPRAYZET brand, Twin Tech offers a comprehensive range of high-performance industrial maintenance aerosols and bulk consumables for various industrial applications, including:

  • Cleaners & Degreasers
  • Rust Removers & Rust Protectors
  • Protective Coatings
  • Injection Moulding Sprays
  • Lubricants
  • Various Industrial Bulk Consumables

SPRAYZET products are widely used in automotive, engineering, plastic, textile, manufacturing, and other industrial sectors, delivering reliable performance and operational efficiency.

EVERSHINE

The EVERSHINE brand presents a complete range of professional car care solutions — from two-wheelers to all types of four-wheelers.

Our premium car care products are specially formulated to clean, protect, and enhance vehicle surfaces. Designed for both interior and exterior applications, EVERSHINE Car Care products provide long-lasting shine, superior protection, and a well-maintained appearance.

Twin Tapes

Under Brand Twin Tapes. Twin Tech manufactures high-quality self-adhesive tapes suitable for industrial, commercial, and household applications.

Our adhesive tapes are engineered to deliver:

  • Strong bonding strength
  • Durability
  • Consistent performance
  • Reliable adhesion across various surfaces

Twinzy

Under the Twinzy brand, Twin Tech offers a range of home care products suitable for residential and commercial spaces.

Twinzy products combine effective odor control with refreshing and inviting fragrances, ensuring a pleasant and delightful environment in every space.

In addition to our own brands, we also undertake third-party contract manufacturing and aerosol packaging, offering complete private label solutions tailored to our clients’ requirements. From formulation to filling, labeling, and final packaging, we provide end-to-end support with confidentiality and quality assurance.

Our commitment to excellence and continuous improvement enables us to serve diverse industries across domestic and international markets with reliability and efficiency.

Our 25-year journey reflects our dedication, reliability, and vision to grow as a trusted name in aerosol sprays and adhesive solutions.

Twin Tech India Pvt. Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of high-quality Industrial aerosol sprays, car care products, self-adhesive tapes and home care products for various industries worldwide.
